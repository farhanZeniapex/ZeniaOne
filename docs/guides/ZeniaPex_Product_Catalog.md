# ZeniaPex Product Catalog

1. ZeniaHR — workforce, HR and payroll management.
2. Zenia Fleets — fleet, vehicle, driver, trip and transport operations.
3. Tender Guruji — government tender and procurement intelligence.

ZeniaOne is the AI voice-agent and telephony platform in this ecosystem. Product-specific features, pricing and contact details must be answered only when the corresponding verified product documentation is available.
